Song analysis

The AI helper is a separate program (the ai-helper folder) that runs chord, beat and key models on your computer, and splits songs into instruments for Jam Mode › Instruments. If it isn't running, the built-in engine is used.

Audio input (guitar)

    About ASIO: the app's audio runs inside a browser engine, which uses your system audio (WASAPI on Windows) and can't load ASIO drivers. For the least delay, pick Lowest latency, match the sample rate, and set a small buffer (64–128 samples) in your interface's own control panel.

    🤖 AI Coach setupInstall Qwen, connect the coach and check it works

    The coach runs Qwen on your own computer through Ollama, so your questions never leave it. Setup takes about ten minutes, most of it downloading the model. Everything else in the app works without it.
